## 📊 What Was Implemented

### ✅ Phase 1: Pain Points Section (NEW)
**10 Problem Cards** showing what can go wrong when buying property:

1. **Ownership / Title** - Does the seller have the right to sell?
2. **Document Mismatch** - Small mismatches can become major problems
3. **Land Category / Usage** - Not every use is possible on every land
4. **Encumbrance / Loan / Charge** - Any charges on the property?
5. **Litigation / Stay** - Any disputes or stay orders?
6. **Area / Gata / Boundary** - Ground area matches documents?
7. **Broker Promises** ⭐ FLAGSHIP - Is there evidence for broker claims?
8. **Price / Value Confusion** - What is the right price?
9. **Documents Scattered** - So many documents, understand what?
10. **Who to Ask** - Problem understood, but who to consult?

**Design Features:**
- Animated cards with staggered reveal
- Document comparison demo (✓ Match / ⚠ Review Required)
- Document flow visualization (UPLOAD → EXTRACT → COMPARE → FLAG → REPORT)
- Important notes and disclaimers
- CTAs to relevant services
- Flagship highlight on Broker Promises card

**Key Messaging:**
- Educational, not fear-mongering
- "We do not sell fear. We sell clarity."
- Professional disclaimers throughout
- Clear distinction between findings and legal conclusions
